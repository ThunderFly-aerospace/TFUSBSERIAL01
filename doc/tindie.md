Simplify your UAV maintenance with the **TFUSBSERIAL01**, the essential tool for debugging and servicing UAV systems. This USB-C to serial converter is fully compliant with the [Pixhawk DS-009 connector standard](https://github.com/pixhawk/Pixhawk-Standards/blob/master/DS-009%20Pixhawk%20Connector%20Standard.pdf), ensuring seamless integration with your avionics equipment.

#### **Key Features:**
- **Dual Connection Flexibility:** Easily connect to both peripheral or FMUs without the need for special RX/TX crossover cables.
- **Simultaneous Communication:** Capable of sniffing serial communication on telemetry links, utilizing both connectors at once.
- **Advanced Safety Features:** Includes excess current protection for USB power and a reverse diode to prevent accidental power flow from FMU to USB.
- **Hardware Flow Control Support:** Ensures reliable data transfer, crucial for critical debugging tasks.

#### Easy Usage
Connect the FMU or peripheral device to the corresponding Pixhawk connector and utilize standard serial terminal tools for effective communication.

#### Effortless Self-Testing
Test cable functionality by connecting the "to FMU" and "to Peripheral" connectors, simulating a local echo for straightforward self-diagnostics.
